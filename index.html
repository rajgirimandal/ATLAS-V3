02 / TEACHER BRAIN

ONE BRAIN.
MANY INTELLIGENCES.

The ATLAS Teacher Brain connects understanding, lesson context, conversation, teaching decisions, responses and memory into one continuous system. The goal is not simply to generate answers, but to manage the process of teaching.
